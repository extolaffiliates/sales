# LinkedIn Agent: Loom Script

**Length:** about 2:30. Don't go past 3:00.
**Format:** screen plus camera bubble (bottom-left, medium size).
**Loom title:** *I haven't opened LinkedIn in [11] days*
**Tone:** you showing a friend something you built. You're not pitching.

> Anything in [brackets] is a placeholder. Swap in your real numbers, or cut the line. A made-up number on camera is the fastest way to sound fake.

---

## 0:00 to 0:06: Hook

**SCREEN:** Claude is already open. You type: *"how did my LinkedIn campaigns do this week?"* and the answer starts loading.

**SAY:**
> So I haven't actually opened LinkedIn in about [11] days.
> This is how I check on it now.

*(Stop talking. Let the answer finish loading on screen. The silence does the work.)*

---

## 0:06 to 0:30: The story

**SCREEN:** Stay on the answer. Scroll slowly over the numbers.

**SAY:**
> For a long time I did LinkedIn the normal way. Like, forty tabs open. Copy-pasting connection notes. Forgetting who I'd already followed up with.
>
> LinkedHelper fixed part of that. But I was still sitting in the app clicking buttons every morning. I'd basically just moved the manual work somewhere else.
>
> So I hooked it up so I can just… talk to it.

---

## 0:30 to 0:50: How it works (keep it short)

**SCREEN:** A simple 3-box sketch: **You → Claude → LinkedHelper → LinkedIn**. Excalidraw or a hand-drawn note is fine. Hand-drawn actually reads as *more* real here.

**SAY:**
> Super quick version of how it works.
> LinkedHelper does the actual LinkedIn stuff. Claude talks to LinkedHelper through something called MCP. Think of it as a plug that lets Claude use the tool directly.
>
> Which means I don't learn a dashboard. I just type what I want.

---

## 0:50 to 1:50: The demo (three asks, one per beat)

Only show things that work live on your account today. If one of these isn't real yet, cut it. Don't fake it.

**Ask 1: Check status**
**SCREEN:** Type *"which campaign is running right now and how many invites went out today?"*
**SAY:**
> First thing I do most mornings. One question, and I get the whole picture. No logging in.

**Ask 2: Start a campaign**
**SCREEN:** Type *"start the [founders-in-London] campaign"*. Show it confirm.
**SAY:**
> Want to start something? I just say which one. It started.
> *(beat)* That's honestly the whole thing.

**Ask 3: Switch or pause**
**SCREEN:** Type *"pause that and switch to the [agency-owners] one instead"*.
**SAY:**
> And if I change my mind halfway through the week, same thing. Pause it, switch it. Takes about four seconds.

---

## 1:50 to 2:15: The part that actually matters

**SCREEN:** Back to your camera. Make the bubble bigger, or switch to camera-only for this bit.

**SAY:**
> Here's what I think the real point is.
> It's not that it sends invites. Every tool sends invites.
>
> It's that I'm not the bottleneck anymore. The outreach doesn't stop on the days I'm busy, or the days I just forget. bc there's nothing to forget. I just ask.

---

## 2:15 to 2:30: Close (soft, one line)

**SCREEN:** Camera.

**SAY:**
> Anyway, that's it. If you're doing LinkedIn by hand right now and want to see what this looks like on your own account, just reply and I'll walk you through it.
>
> Talk soon.

*(Stop recording. No outro or logo. Don't add "smash that like button" either.)*

---

## Recording checklist

**Before you hit record**
- [ ] **Blur or hide prospect names and messages.** Those are real people's details. Use a test campaign or Loom's blur tool.
- [ ] Pre-type nothing. Typing live is part of what makes it believable. Do have the three asks written on a sticky note.
- [ ] Close every other tab. Turn on Do Not Disturb. Zoom the browser to about 125% so text reads on a phone.
- [ ] Do a full dry run once so you know how long each Claude answer takes to load.

**While recording**
- [ ] **Don't read this word for word.** Read it twice, then record from the bold beats only. Talking from memory is what keeps it from sounding AI-written.
- [ ] Leave the "um"s and the small restarts in. They're what make it sound human.
- [ ] Move the cursor slowly. Point at the thing you're talking about.
- [ ] If the first 6 seconds feel flat, re-record just those. They decide whether anyone watches the rest.

**After**
- [ ] Trim dead air at the start and end only. Keep the pauses in the middle.
- [ ] Set the thumbnail to the frame where the Claude answer is on screen, with your face visible.
- [ ] Turn on Loom's captions. A lot of people watch on mute on LinkedIn.

---

## Why it's built this way

- **The outcome comes first, then you.** About 30% of viewers drop off in the first 30 seconds, so the hook shows the result before any intro. No "Hi, I'm…".
- **2 to 3 minutes.** That's the range where demos keep roughly half their viewers.
- **Screen plus camera.** A real face beats a faceless walkthrough for trust.
- **Three asks, not ten.** One clear job per beat. "It can do anything" is a claim. Three things done live is proof.
- **The CTA is one sentence and it's a favour, not a pitch.** That's the Nick pattern. The persuasion is done before the ask shows up.
