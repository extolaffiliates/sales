# CTA and P.S.

**Load:** `agents/cold-email/knowledge/06-cta.md`, `07-psychology.md` (foot in the door, detachment, Zeigarnik).

## Steps
1. **Know the stage:**
   - **Email 1:** interest, offer or confirmation.
   - **Email 3:** lower friction ("if I made a video for you, would you watch it?").
   - **Email 4:** redirect.
   - **After a positive reply:** specific times.
2. **Match the offer:**
   - Free work → offer CTA ("Want me to send the 3 ideas?").
   - Pay-per-result → risk minimizer ("If I could {result} {risk minimizer}, would you be interested in speaking?").
   - Strong pain line → "Solve" ("Is reducing {pain} this quarter worth a quick chat?").
   - Problem sniffing → confirmation ("Is this still the case?").
3. **Write 3 CTAs of different types.** Mark which is the control (interest) and which is the test (offer, or no CTA).
4. **Checks:**
   - It's the only question in the email.
   - It can be answered in 5 words or fewer.
   - No time slots, calendar links or "15 minutes" in email 1.
   - No "let me know if you're interested", "would love to", "hop on a call".
5. **Pressure release (optional):** "Either way, thanks for reading." or "If not, no worries."
6. **P.S.:** add one if the body is short or a second hook would help. Pick a type: poke the bear, future pacing, deposit, testimonial, friction remover, case-study stack, or opt-out (`06`).

## Output
- 3 CTA options with their type and stage
- the recommended P.S. (or "none")
- a one-line rationale, citing the evidence (e.g. Gong interest vs time)
