# Cold email offer

**Load:** `cold-email/knowledge/02-offers.md`, `07-psychology.md` (reciprocity, loss aversion, foot in the door). **Libraries:** `offer-library.md`, `proof-bank.md`.

## Steps
1. **State the current offer in one sentence.** If you can't, that's the problem.
2. **"Why wouldn't they reply?"** List the 5 hidden objections:
   - effort or time to switch
   - "is it really better?"
   - "who else got this, and are they like me?"
   - the risk of failure (to them personally)
   - "we already have someone"
3. **Problem-awareness check.** Does the prospect *know* they have this pain?
   - If not, choose a **reveal-the-problem** offer (O2, O4, O11). Don't offer an audit for a pain they don't feel; Eric's DMARC audit flopped.
4. **Pick an offer rung** (`02`) and 2–3 candidates from `offer-library.md` that match the niche and the proof situation.
5. **Strengthen each candidate:**
   - Define the result ("a meeting held with a decision maker at a company with 10+ staff").
   - Add a number and a timeframe. Optionally test a timeline-hook version.
   - Add risk reversal, or explain why not (Eric's anti-guarantee stance).
   - **Chunk up** (bigger outcome) or **chunk down** (a small annoying job you take off their plate).
   - **Reframe the assumption:** "need an X?" becomes "how do you know your current X is…?"
   - Check for a **straight line to cash** from the freebie to the paid offer.
6. **Test before you build.** Propose a "would you want it?" email before building any lead magnet.

## Output
For 2–3 candidate offers, each with:
- a one-line offer
- its rung on the ladder
- which objections it removes
- the proof or risk reversal it uses
- the niche fit
- a sample CTA line (pass it to `cold-email-cta`)
- a recommended test order

Never promise results Extol can't deliver. Never use "Are you taking on more clients?".
