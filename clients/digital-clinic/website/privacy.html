Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information digitalclinic.ai collects through this website and when you contact us, and what we do with it.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

digitalclinic.ai is run by Amit Chavda, based in Tunbridge Wells, Kent, UK. For anything about your personal information, email hello@digitalclinic.ai.

What this website collects

Very little. This website has no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies. It does not store anything you type.

The contact form does not send anything to us by itself. It opens your own email app with a message filled in, and nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email.

Like every website, our hosting provider may keep short-lived server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site secure and working.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell us about yourself and your company. We use it to:

  • reply to you and arrange a consultation if you want one;
  • carry out any work we agree with you;
  • keep a record of our correspondence.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and, where we work together, the performance of a contract with you or your company.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. If your diagnosis calls for a specialist, we only share your details or our notes with them after agreeing it with you. Our email and hosting providers process data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and for any period required by law, then delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit its use. Email us to make a request. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we add analytics or other tools to this website, we will update this notice first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
