# Offer Library: AI automation agency (Extol)

Rules are in `../knowledge/02-offers.md`; evidence is in report 06 §8. **Always define the result, name a number and a timeframe, and match the offer to the niche's pain.** Put real proof in `proof-bank.md`. Without it, lead with free work or risk reversal.

## Picking an offer
1. **Is the pain obvious to them?** Yes → O1, O3, O6, O9, O10. No → reveal it first: O2, O4, O11.
2. **Are they skeptical of agencies?** → pay per result (O3, O14) or free work (O5, O12).
3. **Is there no proof yet?** → O2, O4, O5, O12 (the work itself is the proof).
4. **Is it a high-value retainer?** → O7 or O10, then the main offer.

**Never:** "Are you taking on more clients?" (Nick Abraham calls it "the worst cold email pitch of all time"). Vague "we help companies grow". Guarantees you can't keep.
