# Hook Bank: fill-in openers by signal and niche

Rules and the hook taxonomy are in `../knowledge/04-hooks.md`. **Use one signal per email.** Every fact must come from a source you actually read.

## By signal
| Signal | Opener template |
|---|---|
| Hiring | `{{first_name}}, saw you're hiring a {{role}}. Usually means {{workload}} is outpacing the team.` |
| Hiring (hedged) | `Saw you're hiring for {{n}} roles. Curious if I'm wrong to guess a few are backfills.` |
| New in role | `{{first_name}}, congrats on taking over {{function}} at {{company}}. Did you inherit {{problem}}?` |
| Funding | `{{stage}} usually means the {{target}} target just doubled. Is {{gap A}} the bottleneck, or {{gap B}}?` |
| Tech gap | `No {{tool}} on your {{page}}. How are you catching {{people}} who get that far?` |
| Tech present | `You're on {{tool}}, so this would sit inside the workflow your team already uses.` |
| Review | `Saw the review from {{reviewer}} mentioning {{issue}}.` |
| AI-search ranking | `Asked ChatGPT "{{query}}" and {{company}} came up {{rank}}, behind {{competitors}}.` |
| Content they published | `Your point on {{topic}} in {{post/podcast}} ({{specific claim}}) stuck with me.` |
| Past customer employee | `Curious if you remember {{us}} from your time at {{client}}?` |
| No signal (honest) | `Most {{segment}} teams your size still {{manual task}} by hand.` |
| Diagnostic question | `When a lead fills in your {{form}} at 9pm, how long until someone calls them back?` |

## By Extol niche (AI automation)
| Niche | Opener |
|---|---|
| HVAC / plumbing / roofing | `Called {{company}} at {{time}} last night and it went to voicemail. In {{season}} that's usually a booked job going to whoever picks up first.` |
| Dental / med spa | `Your booking page sends people to a phone line that's closed after 5. Guessing a chunk of new-patient enquiries land after hours.` |
| Real estate | `Submitted an enquiry on {{listing}} {{days}} days ago and haven't heard back yet. Most buyers have messaged 3 agents by then.` |
| Agencies | `Saw you're hiring an account manager. Usually means client reporting is eating senior time.` |
| E-commerce | `Your abandoned-cart email arrives {{n}} hours later and reads like a receipt. Guessing recovery is below what it could be.` |
| Recruiting | `Saw {{n}} open reqs on your board for {{client type}}. Sourcing usually becomes the bottleneck before closing does.` |
| Legal / finance | `Your intake form asks for 14 fields before anyone calls back. Guessing a few good matters drop off on page one.` |

⚠️ The niche openers above describe **tests you must actually run** (calling, submitting an enquiry, reading their page). **Never claim you did something you didn't do.**
