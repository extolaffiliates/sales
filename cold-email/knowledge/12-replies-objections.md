# 12 · Replies and objections

**Speed:** reply within **1 hour**. That's about 7x more likely to qualify the lead than waiting another hour, and 60x vs 24h+ (HBR) [R06]. Plan on **40–55% of positive replies becoming meetings** [R06].

## Reply labels (Eric's `positive-reply-scoring`, plus Instantly's labels) [R05][R06]
| Label | Counts as positive? | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| `positive_interested` | ✅ | Book it: 2 specific times *or* a calendar link (now a specific-time ask wins: 37%), plus one line restating the value. Under 50 words |
| `positive_soft` ("send info", "reach out in Q3") | ✅ | "Send info": 2–3 lines plus ONE qualifying question, not a PDF. A future date: confirm it and log it |
| `positive_referral` | ✅ | **Thank them genuinely** (gratitude doubles helping) and email the person they named, mentioning the referral |
| `neutral_question` | ½ | Answer briefly, then one question |
| `negative_notnow` | ❌ | "Totally fair. Mind if I check back in {{month}}?" Log the date; come back with something *new* |
| `negative_notfit` | ❌ | One polite line; suppress |
| `negative_hostile` | ❌ (a risk signal) | One-line apology; suppress; watch the rate (pause if above 0.3%) |
| `unsubscribe` (any wording, e.g. "stop", "remove me") | ❌ | Confirm in one line; suppress **immediately and permanently**; never argue |
| `ooo` / `bounce` | excluded | Note the return date or alternate contact; don't reply |

## Objection responses
**Principles:**
- **Acknowledge, ask ONE neutral question, don't argue** (Braun).
- **Mirror their tone and length** (Lavender).
- About half of all pushback is a reflexive brush-off (Gong: 49.5% dismissive) [R06].

| Objection | Response pattern |
|---|---|
| **"Not interested"** | Josh Braun [V-FILE, R04]: "Totally understand. I'm sure you probably get dozens of emails offering {{category}}. And I'm sure you delete most of them. Here's my ask. (And I know it's an ask because you don't owe me anything). Is timing off, or are you highly skeptical that {{approach}} can handle {{their complexity}}?" (reportedly about 50% respond) |
| **"Send me info" / "send an email"** | It's usually a polite no (Braun). Mirror it and qualify: "Happy to. So I don't send you something generic, is {{problem A}} or {{problem B}} more relevant right now?" |
| **"We already have an agency / someone"** | "Makes sense, most teams we talk to do. Out of curiosity, how are you handling {{specific gap}} today?" Treat it as a buying signal |
| **"Too expensive" / "no budget"** | Reframe around the cost of doing nothing, or ask when the budget resets. Offer the smallest first step or a pay-per-result option |
| **"Not now"** | Ask for the month and permission. Offer a light resource in the meantime |
| **"Who are you?" / "How did you get my email?"** | "Thanks for asking. I found your address on {{source}}. I'm {{name}} at {{company}}. If you'd rather not hear from us, reply 'remove' and I'll delete your details. Our privacy notice is at {{link}}." (GDPR Art. 14/21) |
| **Wrong person** | "Thanks, {{name}}, really appreciate it. Who'd be the best person for {{problem}}?" |

**Structural rule:** end on one question, never two [R06].

## Automating replies (Saraev #303) [R01]
1. Load the conversation history and a knowledge base.
2. Classify the reply.
3. Pick the closest pre-approved template.
4. **The AI fills in only the variables.** "Minimal freedom… retain control over tone and quality."

Anything the model can't classify confidently goes to a human. Instantly's AI Reply Agent and Smartlead's inbox can label replies; keep a human in the loop at first [R05].
