## Choosing a framework
1. **Do you have a strong, fresh signal?** Yes → GEX basic, 4T or Safe Space.
2. **Can you scrape their website or content?** Yes → Saraev's icebreaker, creative ideas, or the specificity line.
3. **Do you have killer matched proof?** Yes → Berman's structure, or case-study overwhelm.
4. **No signal, big market?** → Diagnostic question or whole offer.
5. **Tier-1 account?** → Premise or SMYKM, plus multiple channels.

**Variants:** always produce 2–3 variants that differ by **one** variable: framework, offer type, CTA type, or subject. [SRC] sk-experiment-design; [R05]; see `14-benchmarks-experiments.md`.
