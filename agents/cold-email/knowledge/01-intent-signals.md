# 01 · Intent, signals and "why you, why now"

The first line must answer: **why this person, and why today?** (Eric: "First Line: Why you, why now" [R04].) Langer's study shows a *real* reason drives compliance on bigger asks [R03].

## Signal ranking (Harvey [SRC]; GEX triggers [R04][R05])
**hiring > funding > tech gap > tech present > industry problem > nothing**

- Use **exactly one** signal as the hook and save the others for follow-ups. Stacking signals reads as a dossier.
- Never reference a stale signal, such as a filled job post or a raise from 6 months ago.
- **Quote the signal precisely.** cmj-hub's linter requires 3+ words of the public signal verbatim [R05].
- Rule: "**A wrong specific is worse than a right generic.**" [SRC]

## Signal → angle map
| Signal | Angle (not the event itself) | Good first line | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hiring** for a role you support | The workload behind the hire | "Saw you're hiring a second SDR. Usually means the first one is buried." | "Congrats on the job posting!" |
| **New in role** (first 30–90 days) | Their mandate; inherited problems | "when you took over at {{company}}, did you inherit {{problem}}?" (Eric) | — |
| **Funding** (60–90 days after) | The pressure the raise creates | "Post-Series-A usually means the pipeline target just doubled." | "Congratulations on your recent funding!" |
| **Tech gap** | Point at the gap | "No chat widget on your pricing page. How are you catching people who get that far?" | Listing their whole stack |
| **Tech present** | Fits the workflow they already use | "You're on HubSpot, so this plugs into what your team already uses." | — |
| **Bad reviews / problem sniffing** | Quote the specific issue | "I saw the review from Mary mentioning {{issue}}…" (Eric) | Shaming them |
| **AI-search / ranking gap** | Show the gap | "I asked ChatGPT {{keyword}} and you ranked 15th behind {{competitors}}" (Eric) | — |
| **Past employee of a customer** | Familiarity | "Curious if you remember {{us}} from your time at {{client}}?" (Jason Bay) | — |
| **Content they published** | Their own words | Quote their point; Becc Holland's premise bucket #1 | "Loved your post!" |
| **Website visitor** (RB2B etc.) | **Use for timing only. Never mention the visit.** Open on the problem the visited page addresses | — | "I saw you visited our pricing page" |
| **No signal** | Be honest: a true role or industry observation | "Most teams your size still reconcile this by hand." | "Love what you're doing!" |

## Evidence on signals (mostly vendor data, so directional only) [R05]
- **Signal-based outreach** gets 5–18% replies vs 1–3% for generic (Harvey). Other vendors claim 8–25%.
- **Two signals within 30 days** ≈ 50–60% true-positive rate, vs about 20% for one (Zenbee). "New VP + funding" ≈ 4–6x baseline (Prospeo).
- **Job changes:** buyers in their first 30 days in a role convert 3x better (UserGems, 40K prospects).
- **Saturation:** the common signals (funding, new role, hiring) are saturated (ColdIQ). Build signals few others use: reviews, ads, job-posting language, AI-search rankings.
- **Decay:** a signal campaign that got 4% on 500 contacts can fall to 1.5% at 5,000 (Jed Mahrle) [R02].

## Segmentation questions (Mark Colgan) [R04]
- **Triggers:** new role? hiring in the department? attended an event? engaged with an aligned influencer? new tech? new office?
- **Segments:** an industry with a case study? a size band? a vertical with an acute problem? a role tasked with fixing it? ad spend? traffic?
- Aim for **6–10 segments of 80–400 contacts** each, with **one campaign per segment**.

## Seniority by company size [R06]
- Founders and owners reply most (Belkins).
- C-level replies 3x more *positively* than directors (Sales.co), and the gap depends on company size.
- **Default:** under 50 staff, email the founder or CEO. In enterprise, email the director or VP who owns the problem.
