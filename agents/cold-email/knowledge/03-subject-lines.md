# 03 · Subject lines

Ready-made patterns are in `libraries/subject-bank.md`.

## Rules
1. **1–4 words, lowercase.** It should read like something a colleague or customer could have sent [CONSENSUS][R02][R04].
2. **`{first_name}` in the subject is research-backed:** +20% opens, +31% sales leads, −17% unsubscribes [RCT: Sahni et al. 2018, R06]. Use it as the default control: "question for {{first_name}}" or "{{first_name}}".
3. **Specific to them:** their event, product, trigger, or a colleague's name. "Tickets for Break Conference 2015" got 50% opens [R04].
4. **Make it look internal:** a direct report's name or their own project name (Orlob) [R04].
5. **Never:**
   - fake "Re:" or "Fwd:" (deceptive, and about −62% replies [R02])
   - an empty subject (+opens, −12% replies [R02])
   - ALL CAPS, emojis, "!"
   - the word **"AI"** (−17% opens, 30MPC 85M emails [R04])
   - "Curious"
   - "Partnership opportunity"
   - salesy promises
6. **"Quick question"** is banned as a default because it reads as automation (Berman, Eric's skill). **But Eric still uses it as a test control:** "Sometimes, these still outperform our best ideas." [R04] Use it only as the B arm.
7. **Questions in the subject** are a [TEST]: Lavender says −56% opens, older Belkins data says otherwise [R02].

## Three subject strategies (Eric's `campaign-copywriting`) [SRC]
| Strategy | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **A. 2–4 word intrigue** | You have a research signal | "question for {{first_name}}", "{{company_name}} equipment", "saw your post", "cheers to the role" |
| **B. Whole offer (subject + preview)** | Data is thin but the offer is clear | "Ever chase renters to pay on time?" + preview "We built a platform that rewards renters…" |
| **C. Problem indicator** | Problem-sniffing campaigns | "looked you up on ChatGPT", "review from Karen", "starter vs. professional" |

**Check:** could a colleague or customer plausibly send this? If yes, it's good. If it smells like marketing, rewrite it.

## The preview line is the second subject
The first line of the body shows in the inbox preview. Never spend it on "Hi Jason, my name is…". See `04-hooks.md`.

## Follow-ups
- **Emails 2 and 4:** blank subject (they reply in the thread).
- **Email 3:** a *new* subject, starting a new thread with a new angle (Eric) [R04].
