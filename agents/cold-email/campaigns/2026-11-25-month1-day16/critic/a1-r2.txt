```
WEAKEST: why-now — with the icebreaker it is a real, checkable signal (about 17); without it the opener is a generic "If most of your shoots..." that could go to any photographer (about 8).

2. Fix the orphan proof line. "I ran outreach in-house at a London production house." is true (B001) but answers nothing the reader is asking. Tie it to the offer and say why it's free, using the proof bank's allowed angle: "I ran outreach in-house at a London production house, and I'm building case studies with photo businesses, so the list is free." Use only if that is true.
3. Cut "Busy agents list often, so a fresh listing is a good clue who needs photos next." to "An agent who just listed will list again." This brings a real send to about 85 words. For UK recipients, add the data source and a privacy link to the footer, which `11-deliverability-legal.md` requires and the template lacks.
```

The B001 claim checks out against the proof bank. The example icebreaker rows are invented, so in production each one must be a substring-checkable fact from the prospect's page; a wrong count ("14 of 20") would be a hard fail.
