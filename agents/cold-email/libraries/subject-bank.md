# Subject Bank

Rules are in `../knowledge/03-subject-lines.md`. Write in lowercase; 1–4 words; a colleague could have sent it.

| Pattern | Examples | When | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Name** (RCT-backed control) | `{{first_name}}` · `question for {{first_name}}` | Default A/B control | Sahni et al. RCT; Eric |
| **Company pairing** | `{{company}} <> {{us}}` · `{{company}} + {{peer}}` | Partnerships, familiarity plays | Eric |
| **Trigger** | `cheers to the role` · `new sdrs` · `series b` · `{{city}} expansion` | A fresh signal | GEX |
| **Their thing** | `{{event name}}` · `{{product}} line` · `{{company}} equipment` | Product or event specific | Gawley; Eric |
| **Problem indicator** | `looked you up on chatgpt` · `review from {{reviewer}}` · `starter vs. professional` · `after-hours calls` | Problem-sniffing | Eric |
| **Offer, self-selecting** | `ever chase renters to pay on time?` · `more customers like {{case study}}?` · `list?` | Clear offer, thin data | Eric |
| **Colleague name** | `{{direct_report}}'s pipeline` · `{{colleague}} suggested` (only if true) | Makes it look internal | Orlob; Berman |
| **Idea** | `3 ideas for {{company}}` · `idea for {{company}}` | Free-work offers | Eric / Extol |
| **Follow-up thread 2 (email 3)** | `question for {{first_name}}` · `{{customer type}} case study` · `show and tell` | New thread, new angle | Eric |
| **Test-only control** | `quick question` | As the B arm only; banned as a default | Eric (still sometimes wins) |

**Banned:** fake `re:`/`fwd:`; an empty subject; anything with "AI"; `curious`; `partnership opportunity`; "grow your revenue"-style promises; ALL CAPS; emojis; "!".
