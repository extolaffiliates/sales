This notice explains what personal information EffectivePoint collects through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

EffectivePoint Limited is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 03637195). Our registered office is 30 Empire House, Great Cambridge Road, Edmonton, London, N18 1EA.

We are the controller of the personal information described here. You can contact us about anything in this notice at info@effective-point.com.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you use our contact form or request an assessment, we ask for:

  • your name and work email address
  • your organisation and role
  • your answers about your Finance transformation (for example, where you are on the journey and what is driving it)
  • anything you write in your message

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies to follow your visit. The company that hosts the website may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • reply to your enquiry or assessment request
  • arrange and prepare for a conversation you have asked for
  • keep a record of our correspondence

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries and, where you ask us to, taking steps before entering into a contract with your organisation.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to marketing emails unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers who help us run this website and our email, and only so they can provide that service. Where any of them process data outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, as UK data protection law requires.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. If your organisation becomes a client, we keep the records we need for the engagement and for legal and accounting purposes, normally for six years after it ends.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it to you or another organisation. You can also object to us using it.

To make a request, email info@effective-point.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way we use personal information changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
